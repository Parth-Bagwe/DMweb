Who We Are

Our Story

A small shop with a big dream — to bring the soul of Himalayan street food to your neighbourhood, one momo at a time.

Since 2018

Born From a Family Recipe

It all started in a tiny kitchen, with a grandmother's recipe passed down through generations. Our founder grew up eating momos at family gatherings in the hills, and wanted to share that warmth with the world.

We opened our first stall in 2018 with just three varieties and a handwritten menu. Today, we serve over 500 customers a day — but the recipe, and the love, remain unchanged.

Every momo is still folded by hand, every chutney is still ground fresh, and every customer still matters.
